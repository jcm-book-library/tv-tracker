TV Tracker · theme proposal

Twelve shows, twelve months

The app would wear the current month's show theme for the whole month, with a plain Default always one tap away. Each theme borrows only colour, type and shading from its show: no stills, posters or logos, and every show name in the mock-ups is made up. Motifs are small, live in the header only, and never sit over your data.
